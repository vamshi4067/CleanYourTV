# Known limitations

- **Netflix profiles:** the profile picker can lose a title deep link. Provider-targeted URLs and fallback are implemented, but opening the chosen movie after profile selection is not guaranteed. A previous live profile test was skipped. There is no Reopen Netflix pick button.
- **Cross-app Watch Later:** saving works from this launcher's Favourites/picker. It does not capture selection inside other apps or sync their watchlists.
- **Add more:** a curated bundled catalogue, not a search of every provider library. Availability varies by region, app version, and subscription.
- **Match Day:** relies on Willow's public schedule format/connectivity. Open Willow opens the app, not a guaranteed specific stream. Followed teams and Eastern Time are source configuration.
- **Weather:** disabled until local coordinates are configured. No personal location is published.
- **Media:** third-party photographs, show artwork, characters, trademarks, and music have separate rights from the MIT code. Clear rights or replace assets for further distribution or commercial use.
- **Firmware/signing:** Home handling differs by TV. A new signing key cannot update another signer's APK. Keep a working Home and a recovery route.
- **Gallery:** captures span several revisions, so a screenshot can show historical labels or saved example titles. It does not prove current provider/fixture availability.
- **Size/network:** bundled media and weather/fixture HTTPS mean the original small-APK/offline-only claims do not apply.

The Virat shudder and custom photo screensaver were removed. Universal cross-app capture and guaranteed Netflix profile handoff are not advertised as working features.
